## Appendix — pricing and assumptions for the Claude Code Effort Ladder

**Snapshot:** 2026-10-07, models Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1. Recalculate when Anthropic changes prices, subscription weights, the Fable cap, supported efforts, or repo evaluation results.

- API input/output prices per MTok used as the subscription-burn ratio: Haiku 5.5 `$0.10/$0.50` (up to 100k input tokens; `$0.50/$2.50` above); Sonnet 5.5 `$2/$10`; Opus 5.5 `$4/$20`; Fable 5.1 `$10/$50`. Exact subscription weights are unpublished. Source: anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5; anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5; kingy.ai/blog/claude-sonnet-5-5-specs-benchmarks-pricing.
- Fable may consume at most 50% of weekly quota. Planning output multipliers: low `0.5x`, medium `0.75x`, high `1x`, xhigh `1.75x`, max `2.5x`.
- Output-burn index = output price × estimated effort multiplier; full burn also includes input, caching, retries, and follow-up turns. The index assumes equal token counts across models, which is false: Artificial Analysis measured Sonnet 5.5 at $7.60 per task against $5.98 for Opus 5.5 at max effort, despite half the per-token price.
- Taste and Quality are 1–10 estimates informed by public benchmarks (2026-10 snapshot), not repo evals. Taste rewards tone, formatting, and conciseness (shorter is better); Quality rewards accuracy, reasoning, and completeness. No rung earns a 10 without repo measurement. Higher effort lengthens output, so taste sags as effort rises.
- Opus 5.5 beats Fable 5.1 on every benchmark Anthropic published: Terminal-Bench 4.0 66.4% vs 55.8%; FrontierCode 1.1 54.4% vs 50.3%; CursorBench 4.0 57.8% vs 51.8%; GDPval-AA v2.1 1846 vs 1735; OSWorld 2.1 81.8% vs 80.7%. Opus 5.5 at default (medium) effort scores 52.5% on CursorBench, above Fable 5.1 at max (51.8%). Hence `opus + medium` is the value point and Fable keeps only a model-diversity niche.
- Haiku 5.5 is strongly effort-dependent, and Anthropic publishes approximate chart readings only, with no coding benchmark: OSWorld 2.1 ~15% low, ~35% medium, ~55% high, ~72% xhigh; GDPval-AA v2.1 ~1300, ~1450, ~1550, ~1620; Humanity's Last Exam ~25%, ~35%, ~43%, ~46%. At high effort it trails Sonnet 5.5 (OSWorld 80.1%, GDPval 1844), hence quality 5 and the mechanical-only scope. Its price makes effort nearly free, so the rung is pinned at high: the low rung is unusable and the gains flatten between high and xhigh. Above 100k input tokens its price rises fivefold, which a mechanical task should never reach. Claude Code's `haiku` alias still resolves to `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, probed with `claude -p --model haiku`; `implementer-haiku` names the full ID.
- Sonnet 5.5 beats Opus 5 on every listed benchmark and is near Opus 5.5 at max effort (CursorBench 55.5% vs 57.8%; SWE-Bench Pro 81.3% vs 89.9%; GDPval-AA 1844 vs 1846). Its quality is steeply effort-dependent: CursorBench medium 39.2%, high 47.8%, xhigh 53.1%, max 55.5%. Sonnet at high effort therefore trails Opus at medium, and Sonnet at xhigh only matches it at higher per-task burn; that is why Sonnet stops at T3.
